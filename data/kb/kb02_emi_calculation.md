# EMI Calculation Rules
The equated monthly instalment is computed with the standard reducing-balance formula EMI = P x r x (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n - 1), where P is the sanctioned principal, r is the monthly interest rate (annual rate divided by twelve, expressed as a decimal) and n is the tenure in months. Interest accrues on the outstanding principal only, so the interest component of each instalment falls and the principal component rises across the tenure. The first instalment is due on the fifth of the month following disbursal, and where disbursal happens mid-month we collect broken-period interest separately before the schedule begins. A change in the floating reference rate is absorbed by extending or shortening the tenure by default, and the EMI amount itself is revised only when the applicant asks in writing.
