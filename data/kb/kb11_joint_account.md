# Joint Account Rules
A joint account may be opened by up to four holders and the operating mandate must be recorded at opening as either sole, jointly by all, or by either or survivor. Under an either-or-survivor mandate any one holder may operate the account and on the death of one holder the balance passes to the survivor without a succession certificate. Under a jointly-operated mandate every debit instruction needs the signature of all holders, and the account is frozen for debits on the death of any holder until the legal heirs are established. A nomination may be registered for a joint account and only one nominee is permitted; the nominee receives the balance as a trustee for the legal heirs. Changing the operating mandate or adding a holder requires a fresh account-opening form signed by all existing holders together with KYC for any new holder.
