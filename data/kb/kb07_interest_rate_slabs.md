# Interest Rate Slabs
Personal Loan rates run from 10.75 percent to 18.00 percent per annum and are set from the applicant's credit score band, employer category and existing obligations. Home Loan floating rates start at 8.35 percent per annum for applicants with a credit score above 780 and rise by 25 to 75 basis points across the lower score bands. Auto Loan rates are 8.90 percent to 12.50 percent for new vehicles and 13.50 percent to 16.00 percent for used vehicles. Education Loan rates are 9.15 percent to 12.00 percent, and a concession of 50 basis points applies to female applicants. Business Loan rates are 14.00 percent to 21.00 percent, priced against turnover stability and collateral cover. All floating rates are benchmarked to the external repo-linked lending rate and are reset quarterly.
