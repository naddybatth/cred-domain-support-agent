# Credit Score Impact Factors
Repayment history is the single largest driver of a credit score and a single payment reported thirty days past due can pull a score down by 50 to 100 points. Credit utilisation, measured as the outstanding balance against the total sanctioned limit, should be held below 30 percent because sustained high utilisation signals repayment stress even when no payment has been missed. The age of the oldest active account and the average age of all accounts both contribute, which is why closing an old card usually lowers a score rather than raising it. A healthy mix of secured and unsecured credit is viewed more favourably than several unsecured lines alone. Every fresh application generates a hard enquiry that costs a few points for up to twelve months, whereas a customer checking their own report is a soft enquiry with no score impact.
