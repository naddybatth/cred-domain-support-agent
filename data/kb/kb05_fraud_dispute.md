# Fraud Dispute Resolution Process
A customer must report an unauthorised transaction through the in-app dispute form, the 24x7 helpline or a written complaint, and the reporting timestamp is what governs liability. Where the loss arises from a deficiency on our side or from a third-party breach that the customer reported within three working days, the customer bears zero liability and the disputed amount is credited on a provisional basis within ten working days. Where the delay in reporting is four to seven working days, the customer's liability is capped at the transaction value or a slab limit, whichever is lower. We acknowledge every dispute within one working day with a reference number and close the investigation within ninety days. If the investigation finds the transaction to be genuine, the provisional credit is reversed with seven days of prior written notice.
