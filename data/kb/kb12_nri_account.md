# NRI Account Eligibility
A person of Indian origin who has resided outside India for employment, business or an indefinite stay qualifies as a non-resident Indian and may hold an NRE, an NRO or an FCNR account. An NRE rupee account is funded only from foreign earnings, and both the principal and the interest are fully and freely repatriable with the interest exempt from Indian income tax. An NRO account holds income earned within India such as rent, dividend or pension, the interest is taxable and subject to withholding, and repatriation is capped at one million US dollars per financial year on submission of the prescribed chartered-accountant certificate. An FCNR deposit is maintained in a permitted foreign currency for a term of one to five years, which protects the depositor from rupee exchange movement. Students going abroad for study are treated as non-residents from the date of departure and their existing resident accounts must be redesignated as NRO.
