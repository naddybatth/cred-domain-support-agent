# Loan Eligibility Criteria by Loan Type
A Personal Loan applicant must be a salaried or self-employed resident between 21 and 58 years of age with a minimum net monthly income of INR 25,000 and at least six months in the current job. A Home Loan requires the applicant to be between 23 and 62 at maturity, with a property that clears our legal and technical valuation, and the sanctioned amount is capped at 80 percent of the assessed property value. Auto Loan eligibility is assessed on the on-road price of the vehicle and we fund up to 85 percent of it for new vehicles and 70 percent for used vehicles under five years old. An Education Loan needs a confirmed admission letter from a recognised institution and a co-applicant who is an earning parent or guardian. A Business Loan applicant must show two consecutive years of filed income tax returns and a business vintage of at least 24 months.
