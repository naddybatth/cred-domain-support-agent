# Account Closure Process
An account closure request must be signed by all account holders and submitted with the unused cheque leaves and the debit card so that the instruments can be destroyed on record. No closure charge applies if the account has been open for more than twelve months; accounts closed between fourteen days and twelve months attract a closure charge of INR 500 plus taxes, and closure within the first fourteen days is free. All standing instructions, auto-debit mandates and linked recurring deposits must be cancelled by the customer before the request can be processed. The residual balance is transferred to another account nominated in writing, or paid out by demand draft when no alternate account is provided. A credit-card account cannot be closed while any amount remains outstanding or while a dispute is open on it.
