# Minimum Balance Requirements
A regular savings account in a metro or urban branch must maintain an average monthly balance of INR 10,000, semi-urban branches require INR 5,000 and rural branches require INR 2,500. The average monthly balance is the sum of the end-of-day closing balances for the month divided by the number of days in that month, so a single large deposit late in the month does not repair an earlier shortfall. A shortfall attracts a charge of 6 percent of the gap between the required and the maintained balance, subject to a floor of INR 150 and a ceiling of INR 600 per month. Basic savings bank deposit accounts, salary accounts with a credit in the last three months, and accounts of minors and senior citizens above 70 are exempt from the requirement. We send an alert by SMS and email when the running average falls below the threshold with seven days left in the month.
