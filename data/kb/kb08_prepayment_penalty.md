# Prepayment and Foreclosure Penalty Rules
A floating-rate loan taken by an individual borrower for a non-business purpose carries no foreclosure or part-prepayment charge at any point in the tenure. A fixed-rate Personal Loan attracts a foreclosure charge of 4 percent of the outstanding principal if closed within the first twelve months, 3 percent between months thirteen and twenty-four, and 2 percent thereafter. Part-prepayment on a Personal Loan is permitted only after six instalments have been paid, is capped at 25 percent of the outstanding principal in a financial year, and by default reduces the tenure rather than the instalment. Business Loans carry a flat foreclosure charge of 4 percent of the outstanding principal unless the closure is funded from the borrower's own verified sources. Every prepayment is applied to principal first and is confirmed with a revised amortisation schedule within three working days.
